FROM DISCOVERY TO YOUR BLOG

Keep the good stuff.
Skip the extra steps.

QuickPost brings a focused composer to Chrome. Start from the popup, or right-click an image, a link or selected text.

  1. 01

    Catch the spark.

    Choose images from your computer, bring in a link, or start with your own words. A context-menu selection opens in the editor for you to review.

    ↗
  2. 02

    Make it yours.

    Add a caption and tags. Pick a blog from your connected Tumblr account. Nothing is posted just because you clicked a context menu.

    ✎
  3. 03

    Find its moment.

    Publish immediately, add it to Tumblr’s native queue, or save it as a Tumblr draft to finish later.

    ◷

A SMALLER FOOTPRINT

Your Tumblr password stays with Tumblr.

The extension uses Tumblr OAuth. Posts go directly to Tumblr’s API; a Cloudflare Worker is prepared only for protected OAuth operations. Preferences stay in Chrome storage.

Read the pre-release privacy policy ↗

A calmer way to share
